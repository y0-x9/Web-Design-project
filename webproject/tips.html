Advising tips

Plan each semester with fewer surprises

Use these guidelines before registration and before meeting your academic advisor.

Registration Checklist

  • Review prerequisites before choosing advanced courses.
  • Balance difficult programming and math courses across semesters.
  • Keep a realistic credit load based on your current GPA and schedule.
  • Meet your advisor before finalizing registration.
  • Save backup course options in case a section is full.
