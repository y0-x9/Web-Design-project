About the system

A focused advising tool for university students

The system helps students organize classes, understand course dependencies, and prepare for registration with more confidence.

Purpose

Many students face confusion when choosing courses because study plans, prerequisites, and credit limits are often checked manually. This website collects the most important planning information in one place.

  1. Reduce registration confusion.
  2. Help students plan semester loads.
  3. Show prerequisite relationships clearly.
  4. Support better academic advising decisions.

Target Audience

The design is simple, readable, and useful for repeated academic planning tasks.

Undergraduate Students

Students can track progress and prepare questions before meeting an advisor.

Freshmen

New students can understand which courses are usually taken first.

Academic Advisors

Advisors can use the information entered by students to guide discussions.
